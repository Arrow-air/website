# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: February 2026*

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Arrow ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website.

We keep it simple: we collect minimal data to improve our site and don't sell your information.

## What We Collect

### Information You Provide
- Contact information if you reach out to us (email, name)
- Any information you voluntarily submit through forms

### Automatically Collected Information
- Basic analytics data (pages visited, time on site, general location by country/region)
- Device type and browser information
- Referral source

We use privacy-respecting analytics that don't track you across the web.

## How We Use Information

- To improve our website and understand what content is useful
- To respond to your inquiries
- To send updates if you've opted in

## What We Don't Do

- Sell your personal information
- Share data with third-party advertisers
- Track you across other websites
- Create detailed personal profiles

## Cookies

See our Cookie Settings for details. In short: we use minimal, functional cookies and optional analytics cookies with your consent.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Request access to any personal data we hold
- Ask us to delete your information
- Opt out of analytics tracking
- Contact us with questions

## Data Retention

We retain analytics data in aggregate form. Any personal information you provide (like contact emails) is kept only as long as necessary and deleted upon request.

## Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. We'll note the date at the top.

## Contact

Questions? Reach out to us through our official channels.
