# Cookie Settings

*Last updated: February 2026*

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help sites remember things and understand how people use them.

## Our Approach

We use minimal cookies. No creepy tracking, no advertising networks, no selling data.

## Cookie Types We Use

### Essential Cookies
**Always active**

These are necessary for the site to function:
- Session management
- Security features
- Your cookie preferences

You can't opt out of these (the site wouldn't work), but they don't track you.

### Analytics Cookies
**Optional — your choice**

If you consent, we use privacy-respecting analytics to understand:
- Which pages are popular
- How people find our site
- General visitor counts and trends

We use this to make the site better. This data is:
- Aggregated (we see trends, not individuals)
- Not shared with advertisers
- Not used to track you elsewhere

**You can decline these entirely** — the site works fine without them.

## Managing Your Preferences

- **On first visit:** We'll ask your preference
- **Change anytime:** Use the cookie settings link in our footer
- **Browser settings:** You can also block cookies in your browser

## Third-Party Cookies

We minimize these. If we embed external content (like videos), those providers may set their own cookies. We try to use privacy-respecting embeds where possible.

## Questions?

Reach out through our official channels if you have concerns.
