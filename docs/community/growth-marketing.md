# Growth & Marketing

Arrow grows by showing the work. Every flight test, CAD change and workshop session is something a future contributor might see and think "I could help with that". Most people who find Arrow today arrive through a clip or a post, so getting real footage of the build in front of the right people is how the community grows.

Arrow's social media and content are run as the **Media & Social Comms** project, listed on [Active Projects](/docs/governance/active-project-list) with its own [project repo](https://github.com/Arrow-air/project-social-comms).

## Where to follow Arrow

| Channel | What's there |
|---|---|
| [X](https://x.com/ArrowAir_) | Build progress, flight tests and replies to the wider drone and eVTOL community |
| [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@arrow-air) | Longer build and test videos, plus quick clips on [Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/@arrow-air/shorts) |
| [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/arrowair) | Milestones and project news |
| [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) | Where the community works, and the first place new things get shared |

## How you can help

You don't need to be a marketer to help Arrow grow.

- **Film what you're working on.** A phone video of a test, a print coming off the bed or a part being assembled is often the best content Arrow has. Share it in Discord with a sentence about what it shows.
- **Tag Arrow when you post about your work.** If you write or post about something you built for Arrow, tag [@ArrowAir_](https://x.com/ArrowAir_) so it can be shared from the main account.
- **Share and reply.** Reposts and thoughtful replies from contributors put Arrow in front of people the main account can't reach on its own.
- **Bring people in.** If you know someone who would enjoy the work, send them to [Getting Started](/docs/overview/getting-started).

For logos, colours and usage rules, see the [Brand Guidelines](/docs/contributing/arrow-brand).
