## Put yourself on the map

The map is opt-in: you only appear on it by registering yourself through the
Discord bot. Location granularity is your choice. Country is enough, and a
city makes your dot precise.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the command">
    In any channel on the [Arrow Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow), type:

    ```
    /new-contributor
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your location">
    Choose your **country** from the list. Optionally add your **city or town**
    by typing and picking from the autocomplete. City is optional on purpose: if
    you'd rather not share it, your dot is placed in the general area of your
    country instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your disciplines">
    Select up to **3 disciplines**, the things you actually work on, from the
    [contributor disciplines list](/docs/community/disciplines) — anything from
    CAD to Composites to Community. They show as tags on your profile card, and
    they're the same tags bounties use, so work can find you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a short blurb">
    One line about what you do (140 characters max). No links needed, since your
    Discord and GitHub are linked from the card already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your GitHub username">
    Optional, but recommended if you contribute code or CAD. The bot knows who
    you are on Discord, but it can't work out your GitHub handle from that, so
    this is where you tell it. It becomes the GitHub link on your profile card
    and the avatar fallback if your Discord avatar can't be used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and wait for the merge">
    The bot snapshots your Discord avatar and opens a pull request with your
    entry. Once a maintainer merges it, your dot is live on this page. Change of
    plans later? Run `/new-contributor` again to update your entry, or
    `/remove-me` to come off the map entirely.
  </Step>
</Steps>
