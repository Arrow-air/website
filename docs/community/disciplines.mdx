# Contributor Disciplines

Arrow keeps one shared list of **contributor disciplines**: the kinds of work
people actually do here. The same tags show up in three places:

- **[Bounty board](/bounty)** — bounties are tagged with the disciplines they
  need, and the board's sections are the categories below.
- **[Contributor map](/docs/community/contributor-map)** — your profile card
  shows up to 3 disciplines that you pick when you register.
- **Project tooling** — matching open work to the people who can do it.

Because everything reads from the same list, the tags always line up. A bounty
tagged **Manufacturing: Composites** is asking for exactly what a contributor
who registered Composites offered.

## The taxonomy

Nine categories, each holding a handful of disciplines. The tags describe the
kind of work, not seniority or specific tools. They're broad on purpose, so
they stay useful as projects change.

## How it's used

**Tagging a bounty.** The [bounty issue form](/bounty/creating-bounties) lists
every discipline as a checkbox. Tick the ones a contributor needs to do the
work — most bounties need one to three.

**Registering on the map.** The `/new-contributor` Discord command asks for up
to 3 disciplines. Pick the ones you want to be found for, because that's how
other contributors discover you.

**Changing the list.** The taxonomy is community-approved and versioned, and it
moves slowly on purpose: bounties, profiles, and tooling all reference it.
Additions or renames go through community review, not a quick PR. Think a
discipline is missing? Raise it in the
[Arrow Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow).
