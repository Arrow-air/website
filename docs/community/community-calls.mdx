Arrow runs several recurring calls where contributors sync up, share progress, and review designs together. No agenda required to join — just show up.

## Weekly Engineering Sync

**Tuesdays at 18:00 UTC** — voice chat on Discord.

This is the main weekly touchpoint. Expect project updates, technical discussions, and problem solving. The tone is casual but focused. If you've been heads-down on something and want to share progress, or have a question that's easier to talk through than type — this is the place for it.

Can't make it? Recordings are posted afterward so you can catch up on your own schedule.

## Design Reviews

Design reviews are scheduled as needed — usually when a significant CAD model or hardware component is ready for feedback before moving to fabrication.

We jump into VR or screenshare to walk through the geometry together. It's surprisingly effective. Rotating a 3D model in real time, pointing at features, asking questions — it beats looking at screenshots by a lot. Anyone in the community can join or request one.

<DemoImage src="/img/community-calls_design-review.png" alt="Arrow CAD design review in VR or screenshare" caption="Design reviews use VR or screenshare — request one anytime a design is ready for eyes" />

## Everything Else

Most coordination happens async in Discord text channels. There's no expectation of being online at a particular time — Arrow contributors are spread across time zones and async is the default.

The calls are here for things that benefit from real-time discussion. But you won't miss anything critical by skipping one — decisions that matter get written up on the Forum or in GitHub.

[Join Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) and check the Events tab for upcoming calls.
