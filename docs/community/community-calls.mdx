Arrow runs recurring voice calls on Discord where contributors sync up, share progress, and review designs together. No agenda required to join, just show up. The [Events tab in Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) is the source of truth for times, shown automatically in your timezone; the times below are UTC and can shift by an hour with daylight saving.

<DemoImage src="/img/community-calls_discord-events.png" alt="The Events tab in the Arrow Discord sidebar" caption="The Events tab in the Arrow Discord is always the up-to-date schedule, in your own timezone" />

## Community-wide calls

**Weekly Arrow Tagup** · Mondays, 18:00 UTC, in voice-chat. The casual all-hands: small updates from every corner of the project and a hangout with the whole Arrow community. If you only join one call, join this one.

**Community Welcome Call** · Mondays, 16:00 UTC, in voice-chat. For newcomers: meet the community, get your questions answered, and find out where you can plug in. Sign up via the Typeform linked on the Discord event so we know you're coming.

## Project calls

Each active aircraft project runs its own weekly meeting in its project voice channel. Anyone can sit in, and lurking to learn how a project works is entirely normal.

## Design Reviews

Design reviews are scheduled as needed, usually when a significant CAD model or hardware component is ready for feedback before moving to fabrication.

We screenshare and walk through the geometry together: rotating the model in real time, pointing at features, and asking questions beats trading screenshots by a lot. Anyone in the community can join or request one.

## Everything Else

Most coordination happens async in Discord text channels, since Arrow contributors are spread across time zones and [async is the default](/docs/community/working-async).

That said, if you can make the calls, make them. They're the best way to stay in the loop with what's actually going on, ask questions while the right people are in the room, and set up a discussion that would take days to arrange in text. Nobody's taking attendance, and decisions that matter still get written up on the Forum or in GitHub, so missing one never leaves you stranded. But regulars will tell you the calls are where Arrow clicks.

[Join Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) and check the Events tab for upcoming calls.
