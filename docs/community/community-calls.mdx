## Design Reviews

Design reviews are scheduled as needed — usually when a significant CAD model or hardware component is ready for feedback before moving to fabrication.

We jump into VR or screenshare to walk through the geometry together. It's surprisingly effective. Rotating a 3D model in real time, pointing at features, asking questions — it beats looking at screenshots by a lot. Anyone in the community can join or request one.

## Everything Else

Most coordination happens async in Discord text channels. There's no expectation of being online at a particular time — Arrow contributors are spread across time zones and async is the default.

The calls are here for things that benefit from real-time discussion. But you won't miss anything critical by skipping one — decisions that matter get written up on the Forum or in GitHub.

Calls are open to anyone — just join the server and turn up. Questions are welcome during the call (save them for the end if possible) or drop them in the voice channel chat whenever. Recordings are posted when available.
