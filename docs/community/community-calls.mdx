## How to Join

All calls are held on the Arrow Discord server. You don't need a microphone to participate — questions can be typed in the voice channel chat.

- **Listen in**: Join the voice channel at the scheduled time. No camera or mic needed.
- **Ask questions**: Type in the voice channel chat during the call, or save them for the end.
- **Missed a call?**: Recordings are posted in Discord afterward.

## Async Coordination

Most coordination happens async in Discord text channels. There's no expectation of being online at a particular time — Arrow contributors are spread across time zones and async is the default.

The calls are here for things that benefit from real-time discussion. But you won't miss anything critical by skipping one — decisions that matter get written up on the Forum or in GitHub.

[Join Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) and check the Events tab for upcoming calls.
