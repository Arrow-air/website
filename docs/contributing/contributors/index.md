Arrow is built by a global community of engineers, designers, pilots and makers. The profiles in this section are written by contributors themselves: what they work on, what they're good at and what they'd like to be pulled into next. If you're looking for someone to help with a part, a test or a review, this is a good place to start.

## Everyone who has shipped code or CAD

Every person below has landed a commit in one of Arrow's main repositories. Where someone has written a profile, it's linked from their card.

## Add your profile

Anyone who contributes to Arrow is welcome to add a profile, and it helps other contributors know when to involve you.

1. Copy [`_template.md`](https://github.com/Arrow-air/website/blob/staging/docs/contributing/contributors/_template.md) from `docs/contributing/contributors/` in the [website repo](https://github.com/Arrow-air/website) and rename it to your Discord name, for example `yourname.md`.
2. Fill in the sections. Keep it short and personal: a little background, what you've worked on, your main skills and the kind of work you'd like to be involved in.
3. Open a pull request to the `staging` branch. The [GitHub Guide](/docs/guides/github-guide) walks through forking and opening a PR if you haven't done it before.

Once it's merged, your profile appears in the sidebar and is linked from your card above.
