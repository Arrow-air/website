## Brand Colors

Arrow's color system spans six palettes, each with a light and dark scale:

- **Slate** — neutral grays for backgrounds, borders, and text
- **Blue** — primary brand color, used for actions and emphasis
- **Teal** — secondary accent, used for highlights and UI elements
- **Orange** — warm accent for alerts and attention states
- **Purple** — tertiary accent
- **Tan** — warm neutral for softer surfaces
