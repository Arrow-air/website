Anyone can submit an Arrow Improvement Proposal. Here's how.

## 1. Read the existing AIPs

Browse the [Proposal History](/docs/improvement-proposals/proposal-history) to understand what's already been proposed and adopted.

## 2. Discuss your idea first

Before writing a formal proposal, share your idea in the [DAO forum](https://dao.arrowair.com/) or on Discord to gather early feedback. This saves effort if the community has concerns.

## 3. Write your AIP

Use the [AIP template](https://github.com/Arrow-air/dao-aips/blob/main/AIP-template.md) in the dao-aips repository. A complete AIP includes:

- **Abstract** — A short summary of the proposal.
- **Motivation** — Why this change is needed.
- **Specification** — The detailed technical or process description.
- **Rationale** — Why this approach was chosen over alternatives.
- **Backwards Compatibility** — Any impact on existing systems or processes.

## 4. Submit a pull request

Open a PR against the [dao-aips repository](https://github.com/Arrow-air/dao-aips) with your new AIP file. Follow the naming convention: `AIP-XXX.md`.

## 5. Community review

Your AIP will enter **Review** status. Engage with community feedback on the forum and in Discord.

## 6. Vote

Once the review period closes, the GBC will schedule a Snapshot vote. If the proposal passes, it moves to **Final** status.
