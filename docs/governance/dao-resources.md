# DAO Resources

Everything Arrow does happens in public, spread across a handful of tools. This page is the map: where to talk, where decisions get made, where the money and contracts live, and where the building happens.

## Talk

| Resource | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) | Day-to-day conversation, project channels and community calls. Start with the [Discord guide](/docs/community/discord-guide). |
| [DAO Forum](https://dao.arrowair.com/) | Long-form discussion: proposals, project updates and anything that deserves more than a chat thread. |
| [Calls & Events](/docs/community/community-calls) | The regular calls anyone can join, and how to find them. |

## Decide

| Resource | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [Snapshot](https://snapshot.org/#/s:arrowair.eth) | Where ARROW holders vote. Every AIP and operational decision passes through here. See the [Snapshot guide](/docs/reference/snapshot). |
| [AIP repository](https://github.com/Arrow-air/dao-aips) | The source of truth for Arrow Improvement Proposals. Browse them on the [AIP index](/docs/governance/aips/aip-index). |
| [Voting history](/docs/governance/good-to-know/dao-voting) | Every operational vote since 2022, with results. |
