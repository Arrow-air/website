## Summary

**AIP-001** defines the AIP process itself. Read it before writing a new proposal.

**AIP-002** created the Grants and Bounties Committee (GBC), the body responsible for reviewing and distributing compensation to contributors.

**AIP-003** is the official membership list, tracking AIP Editors and GBC members. It's a Living AIP, updated as membership changes.

**AIP-004** formalized the Time Commitment Grant, the system for ongoing contributor pay. There are five compensation levels and a 1.5× ARROW token multiplier.

**AIP-005** introduced the task/bounty/grant submission framework, including the "Information Note" document format required for all submissions.

**AIP-006** created the Projects Framework: DAO-funded projects with named leaders, monthly budget caps, and required community updates.

**AIP-007** is the living Projects List, amended each time the DAO votes to create or close a project.

**AIP-008** codified Arrow's mission ("increase physical connectedness to the people and places you love") and a five-step product roadmap from small drones to manned air taxis.

**AIP-009** proposed the decentralized manufacturing protocol: an on-chain shop with bonded manufacturers, collateral that can be slashed, and open-source licensing for Arrow-designed products.

**AIP-010** established Tokenomics V1: fixed 100M $ARROW supply with no minting, three active token utilities (governance, manufacturing bonds, contributor compensation), and an annual treasury deployment cap approved by governance vote each year. See [The $ARROW Token](../arrow-token) for the full framework.
