**Arrow is a global community designing, building and flying open source aircraft.** VTOL aircraft, the kind that take off and land vertically without a runway, could do for physical travel what the internet did for communication: shrink distance, and make fast, affordable movement something most people can actually use.

Roads only go where roads go. VTOL aircraft don't need them. With an open, automated VTOL network, people could live anywhere and still be close to the people and places they love. That's the long game, and we're playing it one aircraft at a time, in the open.

## What We Build

We start small and scale on purpose. Each aircraft is bigger and more capable than the last, and each one is built on what the one before it taught us.

- **Project Quiver** is our first completed aircraft: a 25 kg open source quadcopter with a modular payload system, available now as a developer kit.
- **Project Caribou** is a heavy-lift hexacopter of around 200 kg, built to carry about 100 kg of cargo or spray. It flew for the first time in November 2026.
- **Project Spearhead** is our fixed-wing VTOL, aiming for higher speeds and longer range.

## How an Aircraft Gets Made

There's no locked hangar. An idea gets proposed on the forum, designed in public on GitHub, built by contributors and workshops around the world, flown, and written up so everyone can learn from it. Then it goes round again.

<BuildLoop />

## How Arrow Works

Arrow has no offices, no management hierarchy and no closed doors. It's organized as a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO): contributors coordinate online, and the people doing the work help steer it.

**Open source.** Every design file, line of code and document is public. Fork it, audit it, build on it. Nothing sits behind a paywall or an NDA.

**DAO-governed.** Token holders vote on direction, treasury spend and major decisions. No CEO, no board. Learn more in [Governance](/docs/governance).

**Globally distributed.** Engineers, designers, pilots and manufacturers from around the world. Day-to-day work happens on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow), [GitHub](https://github.com/Arrow-air) and the [DAO Forum](https://dao.arrowair.com).

**Open manufacturing.** Arrow doesn't own factories. Anyone can build from the designs, and a network of community manufacturers brings official Arrow hardware to customers. [Here's how that works.](/docs/overview/manufacturing)

Work is organized through [bounties and grants](/docs/contributing/grants-bounties), funded projects, community [proposals](/docs/governance/aips) and self-directed contributions. If you see something worth doing, do it. The full picture is in [How We Work](/docs/contributing/how-we-work).

**Aircraft first. DAO second.** The DAO is how we organize. The aircraft are the point.
