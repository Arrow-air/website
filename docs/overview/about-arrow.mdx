**Arrow is a global community designing and building open source aircraft.** We believe VTOL aircraft (vehicles that take off and land vertically, without runways) could do for physical travel what the internet did for communication: collapse distance and make fast, affordable movement something most people can actually access.

Roads constrain you to where roads go. VTOL aircraft don't. In a world with an open, automated VTOL network, people can live anywhere and still stay connected to the people and places they love. That's the long-term goal. We're building toward it one aircraft at a time, in the open.

## What We Build

Arrow starts small and scales deliberately. Our first completed aircraft, **Project Quiver**, is a 25kg open source quadcopter available now as a developer kit. **Project Spearhead** is our next platform, a fixed-wing VTOL pushing toward higher speeds and longer range.

## How Arrow Works

Arrow has no offices, no management hierarchy, and no closed doors. It's structured as a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO). Contributors coordinate online. All designs are public by default.

**Open source:** Every design file, line of code, and document is public. Fork it, audit it, build on it. Nothing lives behind a paywall or NDA.

**DAO-governed:** Token holders vote on direction, treasury spend, and major decisions. No CEO. No board. Learn more in [Governance](/docs/governance).

**Globally distributed:** Engineers, designers, and manufacturers from around the world. Day-to-day work happens on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow), [GitHub](https://github.com/Arrow-air), and the [DAO Forum](https://dao.arrowair.com).

**Open manufacturing:** Arrow doesn't own factories. Designs are open for anyone to build. A community network of manufacturers brings hardware to market globally. [More on that here.](/docs/overview/manufacturing)

Work is organized through [bounties and grants](/docs/contributing/grants-bounties), community [proposals](/docs/governance/aips), and self-directed contributions. If you see something worth doing, you do it. The full picture is in [How We Work](/docs/contributing/how-we-work).
