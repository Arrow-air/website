# Getting Started

Welcome to Arrow. This page helps you find your footing depending on what you're here to do.

## I'm an Engineer

Browse the project tabs (Quiver, Spearhead) to explore open source aircraft designs, engineering reports, and assembly guides. When you're ready to contribute, check out the [Contributing](/docs/contributing) section and the [Bounty Board](/bounty) for paid opportunities.

## I'm a Designer

Arrow needs UI/UX, graphic, and industrial design contributions. Jump into [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) to find the relevant channels, then check the [Bounty Board](/bounty) for open design tasks.

## I'm here for the DAO / Governance

Read about [how we work](/docs/contributing/how-we-work), then explore the [Governance](/docs/governance) section to understand Arrow's DAO structure, the $ARROW token, and how to participate in decisions via AIPs and Snapshot voting.

## I want to join the community

Come say hi on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/arrow) — it's where everything happens day-to-day. Then read [How We Work](/docs/contributing/how-we-work) to understand how Arrow is structured and where you can make an impact.
