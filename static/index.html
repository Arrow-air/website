Your Open Source Aircraft Community

Arrow is a free-to-join community building world-changing drones and manned eVTOL aircraft.

Our collective efforts are open source and our organization is decentralized and community governed.

"Arrow exists to do one simple, hard thing: make safe, vertical-takeoff flight affordable for everyone. We build in public, reward merit over titles, and learn fast together. If we get this right, point-to-point travel becomes as accessible as a rideshare - everywhere." - Thomasg
WHAT IS ARROW?

Arrow is a hands-on, internet‑native lab

We design in public, build with a global crew, test out loud, and fly real missions.

Every artifact—CAD, code, flight logs—feeds a shared knowledge loop that makes the next iteration safer, faster, and more capable.Jump in where you’re strongest, learn where you’re curious, and help push VTOL from prototype to everyday infrastructure.

Anyone can join our community and contribute.

DESIGN Together

We sketch in public - sharing CAD, simulations, and ideas on GitHub and Discord. Clear standards and open reviews keep momentum high while welcoming first-time contributors and veteran engineers alike.

Build Together

From community kits to local fab partners, parts are sourced and assembled wherever talent lives. Modular interfaces and documented BOMs make upgrades snap‑in instead of start‑over.

Test Together

We validate in the open: bench rigs, tethered hovers, flight logs, and failure reports published for all to learn from. Data, not ego, decides what ships next.

Fly Together

Real missions prove the work. We deploy with partners, gather telemetry, and fold lessons back into the design—each flight making the entire network smarter and safer.

Best idea wins. Build in the open.

Open Source Philosophy

We design aircraft in public. Code, CAD, and tests shared for anyone to inspect, fork, and improve. Openness keeps us honest, attracts talent, and compounds progress. Join the community, propose changes, and ship real hardware faster together.

What Problem Is Arrow Solving?

What Problems Is Arrow Solving?

Arrow is adding another layer of transportation infrastructure to fill in existing gaps or shortcomings within the current global network. This new layer of infrastructure will be VTOL aircraft that can transport people and goods, point to point regionally and locally with speed.

Closed, Siloed Aerospace

Open-source hardware, public design, and DAO governance replace secrecy with transparent collaboration.

Slow Hub‑And‑Spoke Travel

We enable fast, point‑to‑point VTOL routes that skip hubs and shorten regional and local journeys.

High Infrastructure Costs

Decentralized manufacturing and local production networks reduce capital requirements and democratize access to advanced aerospace technology.

Last‑Mile Logistics Gaps

Cargo VTOL closes the distance between depots and doorsteps, reducing delays for time‑critical goods.

Slow Hardware Iteration

Software‑style workflows, simulation, and AI tools compress design cycles and ship safer systems faster.

Fragmented Global Talent

An internet‑native community builds and manufactures everywhere at once, accelerating iteration and scale.

Community Growth

Help Us Grow Our Arrow Community

Arrow relies on organic growth. Anyone in our decentralized can contribute to our social media production and earn Arrow token.

Twitch & YouTube

Stream builds, test flights, and dev tutorials. Share your Quiver mods or code live. Every view grows the fleet and earns you Arrow token.
